    ■ Questions

    Asked by security teams.

    Does any of our data reach you?
    Not when you self-host. Prompts, files, history and memory stay in your database and storage, and model requests go to the providers you configure. A dedicated instance we run is covered by your agreement with us.
    Which models can we use?
    OpenAI, Anthropic, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (including routers and self-hosted servers like Ollama and LM Studio), or Berry Router. Administrators choose which models each team may use.
    Where does agent code run?
    In a sandbox per task, separate from Berry's own services. Administrators decide whether code execution is on, whether it needs approval and whether the sandbox can reach the network.
    How do people sign in?
    With Google Workspace single sign-on, or email and password limited to your domains. SCIM 2.0 keeps users and groups in sync with your directory.
